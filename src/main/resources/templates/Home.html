Terms & Conditions

1. Plan and Pricing
All prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes and approved third-party expenses. The Complete Plan is a one-time setup fee of $1,299 plus a subscription of $120 per month. The domain is charged separately and is not included in the setup fee or the monthly subscription.

2. Setup Scope
Setup includes customization of Growth Multiplier's existing website system, customer branding and supplied content, responsive storefront, consultation form, admin dashboard, booking management, review moderation, service catalogue, gallery configuration, database and initial deployment, SSL, basic technical SEO, two reasonable revision rounds, admin training and 30 days of launch support. Completely original platforms, extensive redesigns and additional functionality require a separate quote.

3. Included Monthly Maintenance
Monthly maintenance includes managed hosting, reasonable database hosting, monitoring, SSL administration, routine backups, security and dependency updates, form and API monitoring, correction of defects in the delivered website, technical support, and up to 30 minutes of minor content or configuration changes per month. Work is measured in 15-minute increments and unused time does not roll over.

4. Corrective Maintenance
An existing feature that worked when delivered but unexpectedly stops working is corrective maintenance. This includes provider-caused broken links, failed forms, admin errors, database or API defects, layout regressions and functionality that no longer matches the accepted specification. Provider-caused corrections do not consume the monthly change allowance.

5. Customer-Caused Repairs
Damage caused by the customer, its employees, another developer, unauthorized code changes, deleted records, incorrect DNS changes, compromised credentials, unsupported software or failure to follow written security instructions is not included and may be repaired as additional work.

6. Minor Changes
Included minor changes may update existing text, contact details, hours, images, links, prices, service descriptions, policies, colors, spacing or configuration. A request is minor only when it does not require a new layout, page, workflow, database structure, integration or substantial redesign.

7. Additional Development
New pages, sections, UI/UX designs, forms, workflows, database structures, admin capabilities, authentication, payments, integrations, calculators, portals, reports, animations, 3D assets, large migrations and major restructuring are additional development. Work is normally estimated at $75 per hour with a $75 minimum and quoted in writing before work begins. Typical planning ranges are $200–$450 for a simple form or section, $400–$800 for a standard page, $650–$1,400 for a custom UI/UX page, $600–$2,000 for an admin feature, and $1,200–$3,000+ for payment functionality. API integrations and major redesigns require custom quotations. Third-party subscriptions, licenses and usage fees are additional.

Growth Multiplier does not independently verify the customer's legal, regulatory, marketing or factual claims.
