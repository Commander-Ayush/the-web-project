Terms & Conditions

1. Scope of Maintenance
The monthly maintenance fee (or the maintenance period included in a Bundle plan) covers: domain name registration and renewal, SSL certificate issuance and renewal, uptime and performance monitoring, routine security patches and dependency updates, and fixing broken links, minor content edits, and small layout bug fixes. Hosting and infrastructure costs are included.

2. Out-of-Scope — New Features
Maintenance does not include new functionality. Examples include, but are not limited to: booking or appointment forms, image carousels or galleries, new pages or sections, third-party integrations (payments, CRM, analytics beyond basic setup), custom design overhauls, or e-commerce functionality. Such requests are scoped and quoted separately based on complexity, and require written (email) approval before work begins.

3. Plan Terms
Flexible: Setup fee due upfront; maintenance billed monthly thereafter. Cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice; setup fee is non-refundable.
2-Year Bundle: One-time payment of $2,800 due upfront, covering setup and maintenance for 24 months from activation. No further payments due during this period unless new features are requested (see Section 2). Non-refundable after 14 days from activation. At the end of 24 months, service continues month-to-month at the standard maintenance rate unless a new bundle is purchased.
4-Year Bundle: One-time payment of $4,800 due upfront, covering setup and maintenance for 48 months from activation. Same renewal and refund terms as the 2-Year Bundle, adjusted for the 48-month period.

4. Payment
Setup fees (or bundle payments) are due upfront before work begins. Monthly maintenance, where applicable, is billed in advance. Failure to pay within 15 days may result in suspension of hosting and domain services.

5. Domain Ownership
The domain is registered and managed on the client's behalf during the active plan. Upon contract end or cancellation, domain transfer to the client's own registrar is available upon request within 30 days; after that, Growth Multiplier is not obligated to retain the domain.

6. Client Responsibilities
The client must provide content, branding assets, and timely feedback or approvals. Delays in client response may extend delivery timelines without penalty to Growth Multiplier.

7. Termination
Either party may terminate with written notice per the plan terms above. Growth Multiplier reserves the right to suspend service for non-payment or abusive use.
