Terms & Conditions

1. Plans, Commitment and Founding-Client Pricing
All prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes, domain charges and approved third-party expenses. The Annual Plan is $1,995 setup plus $199 per month for a 12-month minimum term. The Loyalty Plan is $1,495 setup plus $169 per month for a 24-month minimum term. These introductory setup prices are limited to the first five qualifying customers; standard setup prices are $2,495 and $1,995 respectively.

2. Refundable Service Deposit
A deposit equal to two monthly payments is due at signing: $398 for Annual or $338 for Loyalty. After the complete term is fulfilled and the account is current, the deposit is credited toward the final two monthly payments. It may first be applied to unpaid invoices, approved additional work, third-party charges, customer-caused damage or early-termination amounts. Any remaining refundable balance is returned or credited after the account is settled.

3. Setup Scope
Setup includes customization of Growth Multiplier's existing website system, customer branding and supplied content, responsive storefront, consultation form, admin dashboard, booking management, review moderation, service catalogue, gallery configuration, database and initial deployment, SSL, basic technical SEO, two reasonable revision rounds, admin training and 30 days of launch support. Completely original platforms, extensive redesigns and additional functionality require a separate quote.

4. Included Monthly Maintenance
Monthly maintenance includes managed hosting, reasonable database hosting, monitoring, SSL administration, routine backups, security and dependency updates, form and API monitoring, correction of defects in the delivered website, technical support, and up to 30 minutes of minor content or configuration changes per month. Work is measured in 15-minute increments and unused time does not roll over.

5. Corrective Maintenance
An existing feature that worked when delivered but unexpectedly stops working is corrective maintenance. This includes provider-caused broken links, failed forms, admin errors, database or API defects, layout regressions and functionality that no longer matches the accepted specification. Provider-caused corrections do not consume the monthly change allowance.

6. Customer-Caused Repairs
Damage caused by the customer, its employees, another developer, unauthorized code changes, deleted records, incorrect DNS changes, compromised credentials, unsupported software or failure to follow written security instructions is not included and may be repaired as additional work.

7. Minor Changes
Included minor changes may update existing text, contact details, hours, images, links, prices, service descriptions, policies, colors, spacing or configuration. A request is minor only when it does not require a new layout, page, workflow, database structure, integration or substantial redesign.

8. Additional Development
New pages, sections, UI/UX designs, forms, workflows, database structures, admin capabilities, authentication, payments, integrations, calculators, portals, reports, animations, 3D assets, large migrations and major restructuring are additional development. Work is normally estimated at $75 per hour with a $75 minimum and quoted in writing before work begins. Typical planning ranges are $200–$450 for a simple form or section, $400–$800 for a standard page, $650–$1,400 for a custom UI/UX page, $600–$2,000 for an admin feature, and $1,200–$3,000+ for payment functionality. API integrations and major redesigns require custom quotations. Third-party subscriptions, licenses and usage fees are additional.

9. Content and Legal Responsibility
The customer is responsible for the accuracy and legality of its prices, promotions, claims, privacy information, licenses, testimonials and security-industry representations, and for securing rights to all supplied logos, photographs, videos and other content. Growth Multiplier does not independently verify the customer's legal, regulatory, marketing or factual claims.

10. Third-Party Services and Usage
Hosting companies, Cloudinary, registrars, email providers, payment processors, Google services and external APIs operate independently. Growth Multiplier will reasonably investigate failures but cannot guarantee their uninterrupted availability or continued compatibility. Provider changes, outages, usage overages and required upgrades may result in separate charges after notice where practical.

11. SEO and Support Targets
Technical SEO foundations are included, but rankings, traffic, leads, sales and revenue are not guaranteed. Initial response targets during business hours are 12 business hours for a complete outage, one business day for unavailable booking or admin functionality, and two business days for ordinary requests. Response time is not guaranteed resolution time. Requested after-hours work may be billed at $125 per hour with a one-hour minimum.

12. Domain and Intellectual Property
Growth Multiplier registers, owns and manages the domain, which is billed separately. The customer may use it while the account remains active and paid. Transfer or ownership buyout requires a separate written agreement, settlement of all invoices and any agreed transfer charge. The customer owns its supplied brand materials and business data. Growth Multiplier retains its reusable source code, architecture, backend, admin framework, templates, libraries and development tools.

13. Billing, Late Payment and Suspension
Setup fees and deposits are due before work begins; monthly fees are billed in advance. Recurring charges, contract length, domain fees, taxes and cancellation conditions will be disclosed before enrollment. Overdue accounts may receive notice and suspension of hosting, admin access or support. Suspension does not erase amounts already due or automatically end the minimum commitment.

14. Cancellation and Renewal
Cancellation requires at least 30 days' written notice. On early termination, outstanding invoices are due, the deposit may be applied to amounts owed, and the setup discount may be adjusted by up to $500 for Annual or $1,000 for Loyalty. At the end of the minimum term, the customer may renew, continue at the then-current month-to-month rate, or end service with the required notice.
